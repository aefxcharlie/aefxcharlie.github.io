About Charlie

I'm a Game Developer working in Unity 6 and C# — 2D mechanics, player controllers, physics and collisions, and the scene and prefab workflow that ties a game together. I'm focused on building solid mechanics and polished, optimized player experiences, with the goal of becoming a fully-fledged Game Developer.
